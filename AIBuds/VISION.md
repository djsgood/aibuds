# AIBuds Vision

AIBuds is an AI Employee for small businesses.

It helps business owners:

- Recover missed opportunities
- Never forget a follow-up
- Book more appointments
- Generate more revenue
- Save hours every week through automation

Every feature we build must answer YES to at least one question:

1. Does it make the customer more money?
2. Does it save the customer time?
3. Does it prevent the customer from losing business?
